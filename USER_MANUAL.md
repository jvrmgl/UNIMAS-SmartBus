# UNIMAS SmartBus - User Manual

This manual provides step-by-step instructions for students, drivers, and admins.

## 1. Getting Started (All Users)

### Step 1: Install and Open
1. Install the app on your Android or iOS device.
2. Open the app.

### Step 2: Grant Permissions
1. When prompted, allow location access.
2. If you want notifications, allow notification permissions.

### Step 3: Sign Up (First-Time Users)
1. Tap Sign Up on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your full name, email, and phone number if required.
3. Choose your role (Student, Driver, or Admin).
4. Create a password and confirm it.
5. Tap Create Account.
6. Verify your account if the app prompts for verification.

### Step 4: Sign In
1. Enter your assigned email and password.
2. Tap Sign In.
3. Use Forgot Password if needed.

### Step 5: Know Your Role
- Student: Track buses and view routes, stops, and ETAs.
- Driver: Run trips, update occupancy, and share live location.
- Admin: Manage buses, drivers, routes, schedules, and announcements.

## 2. Student and Guest Guide (Step by Step)

### 2.1 Track Buses on the Map
1. Open the map screen.
2. Find nearby buses and stops on the map.
3. Tap a bus marker to open its info card.
4. Review route, occupancy, and ETA if available.
5. Tap anywhere on the map to close the card.

### 2.2 View Routes and Stops
1. Open the Routes section.
2. Select a route to view its stops.
3. View schedules if provided for that route.

### 2.3 Submit a Report
1. Open the Reports section.
2. Tap Create Report.
3. Choose a category (cleanliness, safety, delay, etc.).
4. Enter a short description.
5. Attach a photo if needed.
6. Tap Submit.

### 2.4 Read Announcements
1. Open the Notifications sheet.
2. Read new announcements.
3. Tap Clear All to mark them as read.

## 3. Driver Guide (Step by Step)

### 3.1 Start a Shift
1. Open the Driver Dashboard.
2. Confirm your assigned bus and route.
3. Tap Start Trip when you begin the route.

### 3.2 Share Live Location
1. Keep location services enabled during the trip.
2. Leave the app running in the background if needed.
3. The app updates your location automatically.

### 3.3 Update Occupancy
1. Use the occupancy controls on the driver screen.
2. Increase or decrease passenger count as riders board or exit.
3. Confirm the displayed occupancy matches the bus.

### 3.4 End the Trip
1. Tap End Trip at the end of the route.
2. Confirm the trip is marked as completed.

## 4. Admin Guide (Step by Step)

### 4.1 Monitor Operations
1. Open the Admin Dashboard.
2. View active buses and overall system status.
3. Use the map to see live bus locations.

### 4.2 Manage Buses
1. Open Manage Buses.
2. Tap Add Bus to create a new bus.
3. Enter bus number, registration, and capacity.
4. Assign a driver and route as needed.
5. Activate or deactivate the bus.
6. From the map, tap a bus marker and use Manage Bus to focus the bus in the list.

### 4.3 Manage Routes and Stops
1. Open Manage Routes.
2. Create or edit routes and route codes.
3. Add or remove bus stops from routes.
4. Update schedules if required.

### 4.4 Publish Announcements
1. Open Announcements.
2. Tap Create Announcement.
3. Enter title and message.
4. Tap Send.
5. Use Resend to send a previous announcement again.

### 4.5 Review Reports
1. Open Reports.
2. Review submitted issues.
3. Update status when resolved.

## 5. Troubleshooting (Step by Step)

### 5.1 Map Not Loading
1. Check internet connection.
2. Confirm location permissions are enabled.
3. Close and reopen the app.

### 5.2 No Bus Locations
1. Confirm the driver has started a trip.
2. Ask the driver to enable location services.
3. Refresh the map screen.

### 5.3 Notifications Not Received
1. Ensure notifications are enabled in device settings.
2. Confirm you are signed in.
3. Check your internet connection.

## 6. Privacy and Safety

- Location is used only for route tracking and ETA accuracy.
- Report data may be reviewed by admins to improve service.

## 7. Support

Contact your supervisor or the project team for help.
